GST Reports
📊

GSTR-1

Outward supplies — B2B (GSTIN-wise), B2C (state-wise), HSN summary. CSV/PDF export for filing.

📊

GSTR-3B

Monthly summary — outward taxable, ITC, net tax payable per CGST/SGST/IGST.

🧾

Credit / Debit Notes

Returns reduce output tax — linked to original invoice, own series.

🏢

B2B vs B2C

Customer GSTIN captured at checkout → correct invoice classification.

📌 GSTIN validation: 15-char format + checksum — FREE, no paid API. Rate changes = DB update, no redeploy. OTP via WhatsApp bridge (free).
